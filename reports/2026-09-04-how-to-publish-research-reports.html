How to Publish Research Assistant Reports

4 September 2026 · sample · publishing

This page is a placeholder so the Research Reports section is browseable. Self-contained HTML reports from the Research Assistant bot belong in the reports/ folder on this GitHub Pages site.

Drop-and-publish

  1. Save the report as reports/YYYY-MM-DD-topic-slug.html.
  2. Append an entry to data/reports.json with title, ISO date, and path. Optional: summary, tags.
  3. Commit and push to main. GitHub Pages deploys; the report appears on the home page and at reports.html.

Do not add reports to data/links.json. Kid tabs and the Arcade stay separate.
